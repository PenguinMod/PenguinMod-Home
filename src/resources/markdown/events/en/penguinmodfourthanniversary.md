Pang has found his old gaming system, and he's been having a blast playing his favorite games! However, he wants something fresh, something new. **So, Pang has decided to recruit you all to remix them!**

This is the **Retro Remix PenguinJam.** In this jam, you will **take an idea from an old game**—ranging from old arcade cabinets to classic mobile apps—and **add your own special twist!**

Maybe a flying bird game, but destroying the pipes instead of dodging them. Maybe the snake runs away from the evil piranha apples. Maybe you play as the enemies trying to defeat a platforming plumber. **The ideas are only as limited as your imagination!**

If you don't know what a PenguinJam is, scroll down!

## Tag

Include the tag **#retroremix** in your project's description! **If you don't, we can't find your project!**

# What is a PenguinJam?

PenguinJam is a game jam! How does a game jam work?

1. **We reveal a theme.** You need to create a PenguinMod project based on the theme. You can make any game that relates to the theme in any way. **It should be a big part of your project.**
2. **Submit a project.** You need to upload your project to PenguinMod **using the tag #retroremix in the description.** Make sure to upload your projects by **October 24th, 2026, at 6:00 PM EST.** Any late submissions will not count.
3. **We rank your projects.** Event managers will go through the submitted projects and rank them based on **Presentation, Originality, Fun, and Theme.** Then, we will sort them by the highest average score.
4. **Awards are given out!** The top 3 projects' creators will get special PenguinJam badges, and everyone who participates gets an Event Participant badge.

## Rules

- Your projects must be **created and published in the duration of the jam.**
- **You are permitted to use Scratch, Turbowarp, or other Scratch mods.** However, your project must work on PenguinMod and be uploaded to PenguinMod.
- **You are allowed to use stock assets and assets you have created before.** You are not allowed to use others' assets without explicit permission. You are allowed to use built in assets in the editor.
- **You are allowed to use code you have made before.** The majority of your project, however, should be mostly new code created in the duration of the jam.
- **You may use any extensions.**

## Teams

- Teams can have **up to 4 members.**
- Designate a **"Team Leader"** that will upload the final project.
- You can **ONLY be a part of 1 team.**
- You may not add new members after submitting.
- **All team members' names must be listed in the project description on upload.**

## Disqualification

Your project will be disqualified if:

- It does not follow the theme.
- It plagiarizes other people's work.
- It is not a game.
- It is not uploaded to the PenguinMod website.
